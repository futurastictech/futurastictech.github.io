❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stages, stars, ink and upgrades are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Why do I go back to stage 1 when I die?

ADVENTURE runs are one-life runs. Your record, stars, ink and unlocked techniques are kept. Once per run you can watch an ad to continue where you fell.

How do I learn the moves?

Open "Moves" and "Practice" from the main menu for every technique and a training area.

My Remove Ads purchase is missing.

Tap "Restore purchases" in Settings, signed in with the same store account.

Can I turn off blood effects?

Yes. Settings lets you reduce or fully disable blood and dismemberment effects.

The game stutters or crashes.

Fully close and reopen the app. Lowering quality in Settings helps on older devices. If it continues, email us.
